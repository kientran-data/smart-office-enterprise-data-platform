# Smart Office Business Scenario

## Company
Doanh nghiệp giả lập có:
- 500 employees
- 10 departments
- 30 positions
- 3 offices
- 30 meeting rooms
- 100 access doors
- 200 IoT devices

## Domains
- HR
- Office
- Access Control
- Meeting Room
- Digital Signing
- IoT

## Business Goals
Hệ thống Data Platform phục vụ:
- Theo dõi nhân sự đi làm
- Phân tích office utilization
- Meeting room utilization
- Digital signing performance
- IoT/device monitoring
- Security/access monitoring
